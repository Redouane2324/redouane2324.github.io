SolPay Africa is building open-source payment infrastructure designed to make Solana payments simple and accessible for businesses and consumers in emerging markets.

What we're building

SolPay Africa aims to provide simple, reusable and open-source payment infrastructure for merchants and developers across emerging markets.

⚡ Fast Payments

Built around Solana's high-performance blockchain for efficient digital payments.

🌍 Africa First

Starting with Algeria and designed with broader African adoption in mind.

🔓 Open Source

Core infrastructure and developer resources will be available to the community.

Core Features

Our MVP focuses on the basic infrastructure required to create a simple Solana payment experience.

🔗 Wallet Connection

Connect compatible Solana wallets to the application.

📱 QR Payments

Generate simple payment requests that can be scanned.

🔎 Verification

Verify payment transactions on the Solana blockchain.
